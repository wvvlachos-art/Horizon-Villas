The house

At the very top of the hillside, Astra takes in the widest views of the lot — the whole bay and the open Aegean beyond. Two floors, three double bedrooms and two bathrooms (one with a soaking tub), a separate living room and a full kitchen, and a run of terraces built for sunset.
